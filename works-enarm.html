Enarm

Simplifying complex medical interfaces for critical care workflows where clarity and speed save lives.

Role

Product Design Lead

Timeline

Q1 — Q2 2024

Services

UX Strategy, System Design, Prototyping

Discuss a project

The Problem

Medical staff navigated dense interfaces under extreme time pressure, increasing risk of human error during critical procedures.

Strategic Approach

We prioritized hierarchical information design and tactile feedback. The interface was rebuilt around the most frequent emergency actions.

Accessibility and high-contrast visibility were non-negotiable constraints from day one.

Outcome

Task completion speed improved by 33%, and error reports related to interface confusion dropped sharply.

Reflection

In healthcare design, every millisecond and every visual cue matters. Precision is empathy.
