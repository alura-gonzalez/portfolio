Safari Nursery

Driving retention through emotional engagement: +3.5pp Day 1 Retention · 65–70% Sustained Engagement.

Role

Product Design Lead

Timeline

Jun - Aug 2025

Services

UX Strategy, Growth Design, Game Design

Discuss a project

The Problem

Traditional nursery management systems fragmented caregiver attention across multiple interfaces. The result was cognitive overload during high-stakes moments of child interaction.

Strategic Approach

We mapped the emotional and operational rhythms of caregivers. The redesign introduces a calm, structured interface that reduces noise and supports quick, confident decisions.

A component-based system ensures accessibility and consistency across every touchpoint, from check-in flows to emergency alerts.

Outcome

Caregivers reported a 35% reduction in navigation time and significantly lower stress during peak hours. The system is now being scaled across three locations.

Reflection

When designing for care, simplicity is not minimalism—it's respect. The interface must disappear so the human connection can remain.
