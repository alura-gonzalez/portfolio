Legal Records Tracker

Building transparency and trust into legal record management through structured, accessible interfaces.

Role

Product Design Lead

Timeline

Q3 — Q4 2024

Services

UX Strategy, System Design, Prototyping

Discuss a project

The Problem

Legal records were stored across disconnected systems, creating audit gaps and compliance risks that impacted team confidence.

Strategic Approach

We mapped every touchpoint in the record lifecycle. The new interface centralizes access while maintaining strict role-based permissions.

A clear audit trail is visible at every stage, reducing ambiguity and supporting faster compliance reviews.

Outcome

Audit preparation time reduced by 50%. Stakeholder trust in record integrity increased significantly.

Reflection

Transparency is not a feature—it's a system. When users can see the full story, they can act with confidence.
