The Lever

Strategic design that moves the needle—aligning product experience with business outcomes through focused, measurable interventions.

Role

Product Design Lead

Timeline

Q4 2024 — Q1 2025

Services

UX Strategy, System Design, Prototyping

Discuss a project

The Problem

The product had strong user engagement but failed to convert that traction into revenue growth. The disconnect between experience and business metrics was unclear.

Strategic Approach

We identified the key lever: onboarding flow optimization. By redesigning the first-run experience with strategic milestones and progressive disclosure, users reached value faster.

Every change was tied to a measurable KPI—activation rate, trial conversion, and feature adoption.

Outcome

Trial-to-paid conversion increased by 22%. The lever framework is now applied to two additional product lines.

Reflection

Not every feature needs to change the world—some need to change the metric that matters. Knowing which lever to pull is strategic design.
