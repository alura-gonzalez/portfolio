Bag Storage

Optimizing storage systems through intuitive product architecture, reducing friction for users who rely on speed and order.

Role

Product Design Lead

Timeline

Q3 — Q4 2024

Services

UX Strategy, System Design, Prototyping

Discuss a project

The Problem

Storage interfaces often force users into rigid taxonomies that don't match real-world behavior. This disconnect creates wasted time and accumulated frustration.

Strategic Approach

We rebuilt the information architecture from usage patterns rather than organizational assumptions. The result is a flexible grid system that adapts to context.

Every interaction was tested for one-hand usability and cognitive load reduction.

Outcome

Search efficiency improved by 28%, and user retention in the storage module increased significantly over the previous quarter.

Reflection

Good storage design is invisible. When users stop thinking about the container, they're free to focus on what matters inside.
